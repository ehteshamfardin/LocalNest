Get started in three steps

Set up a share and connect from another device on your local network.

Install the app

Download LocalNest from the official release link below and install it on your device.

Start the server

Open the app and tap Start Server. Check the address and port shown by the app.

Connect and share

Connect another device to the same Wi-Fi or hotspot, then open the share link.
